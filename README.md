## `$ whoami`

I am an undergraduate pursuing **BS Chemistry**, interested in understanding chemical and physical systems at the quantum and molecular level.

My interests lie at the intersection of chemistry, physics, mathematics and computation. I enjoy exploring how mathematical models and numerical methods can be used to investigate quantum-mechanical systems.

I am particularly interested in developing a deeper understanding of molecular systems, their electronic structure, and the computational methods used to study them.

## `$ selected_projects`

### 01. QuantumBoundStates
**Computational study of a particle in a one-dimensional infinite potential well.**

- Finite-difference discretisation of the time-independent Schrödinger equation.
- Construction and diagonalisation of a tridiagonal Hamiltonian.
- Analytical and numerical energy comparisons.
- Wavefunction validation and grid-convergence studies.

[**Explore repository →**](https://github.com/zekrydon-exe/QuantumBoundStates)

### 02. QuantumHarmonicOscillator
**Numerical investigation of the one-dimensional quantum harmonic oscillator.**

- Calculation of energy eigenvalues and eigenfunctions.
- Validation against analytical solutions.
- Grid and domain convergence studies.
- Wavefunction normalisation and eigenpair residual analysis.

[**Explore repository →**](https://github.com/zekrydon-exe/QuantumHarmonicOscillator)

### 03. HydrogenAtomAndOrbitals
**Computational treatment of the hydrogen atom and its orbitals.**

- Numerical solution of the radial Schrödinger equation.
- Sparse Hamiltonian construction and eigenvalue calculations.
- Validation of energies, radial wavefunctions and orthogonality.
- Three-dimensional orbital probability-density visualisation.

[**Explore repository →**](https://github.com/zekrydon-exe/HydrogenAtomAndOrbitals)
